## The Burchell arc { #the-burchell-arc }

Episodes of the *Real Python Podcast* with the same guest, [Jodie
Burchell](https://realpython.com/podcasts/rpp/), spread across the four years
this field changed shape. Each is assigned in the week it fits, and they are
also meant to be taken as one thing — which is what the [Burchell
reflections](assignments.md#the-burchell-reflections) ask you to do. The one
marked *not assigned* is here so the sequence is complete; listen to it if the
arc interests you.

{{ burchell_arc() }}

Episodes 1 and 2 were recorded four and three months before ChatGPT was
released, two weeks apart. That is the reason they are assigned: they are a
record of what a careful, well-informed person expected to matter, written down
before anyone knew. Everything after them is the same person revising.
